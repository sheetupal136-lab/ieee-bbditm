About IEEE BBDITM Student Branch

Founded at Babu Banarasi Das Institute of Technology and Management, Lucknow, our branch bridges classroom theory with IEEE international engineering standards.

Advancing Technology for Humanity

The IEEE Student Branch at BBDITM Lucknow (STB10214) provides an authentic platform for undergraduate engineers to author peer-reviewed papers, collaborate on hardware rovers and IoT smart grids, and lead regional symposiums.

Under the guidance of Branch Counselor Prof. Rafik Ahmad and institutional patron Mrs. Alka Das, our branch is recognized across the IEEE Uttar Pradesh Section for its vibrant technical activities and student leadership.
