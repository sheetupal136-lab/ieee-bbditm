National Council Honor • 2023 IEEE INDIA COUNCIL

OUTSTANDING STUDENT BRANCH OF THE YEAR 2023 , IEEE INDIA COUNCIL

Awarding Body: IEEE INDIA Council (Received at Hyderabad)

The IEEE BBDITM Student Branch on receiving the “Outstanding Student Branch of the Year Award 2023” from the IEEE INDIA Council.

Earning this prestigious recognition highlights hard work of the student branch members. It’s a testament to their commitment to organizing outstanding events and providing invaluable opportunities for growth and development to their members.

Acknowledging the efforts of the Branch Counselor and the Chair IEEE Student Branch, who went to Hyderabad to receive the award, as well as expressing gratitude to the Hon’ble Chairperson of BBDEG for their support, truly emphasizes the collaborative effort and leadership that contributed to this success. I would like to also thank to Director, BBDITM and IEEE UP section Chair for their guidance and support at every moment. It inspires other student branches to follow suit and make a positive difference in the world through the application of engineering and technology. Well done on this outstanding achievement!

OUTSTANDING STUDENT VOLUNTEER OF THE YEAR 2023 , IEEE INDIA COUNCIL

Awarding Body: IEEE INDIA Council

Congratulations to Mr. Hitansh Dwivedi and the entire IEEE BBDITM Student Branch on this outstanding achievement! It’s a testament to their dedication and commitment to the engineering community.

Recognition like the “Outstanding Student Volunteers of the Year Award 2023” from the IEEE INDIA Council celebrates dedication being acknowledged and celebrated. Kudos to the Branch Counselor, the Chair IEEE Student Branch, and everyone involved for their hard work. The support from the Hon’ble Chairperson of BBDEG is invaluable in fostering such remarkable achievements. I would like to also thank to Director, BBDITM and IEEE UP section Chair for their guidance and support at every moment. This award ceremony not only honors the recipients but also highlights the significance of community service in the engineering domain. Keep up the fantastic work.
